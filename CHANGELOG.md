# Changelog

## v2.3.0 — Stable Introduction and Literature Synthesis Release

- Promoted the independently tested v2.3 RC1 writing rules to stable v2.3.0, maintaining the seven dependencies and existing Results/Discussion gates.
- **Refinement 1:** Introduction openings prioritize a concrete observed economic phenomenon, avoiding abstract aggregate/distributional labels as substitutes for motivation.
- **Refinement 2:** Introduction findings preview is selective and proportionate; omit unnecessary lists of numbers and categories while keeping headline economic contrasts.
- **Cross-paper permanent rule:** robustness, measurement or diagnostic sensitivity is not a standalone numbered economic hypothesis (H4/Hn). Preserve analyses and migrate legacy references consistently with author approval.
- No added executable validator, external Skill dependency, revised numeric results or relaxed evidence requirement. Formal upstream citation-validation status remains separate from editorial pass decisions.

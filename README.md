# Social Science Writing Gate — Economics-Enhanced v2.1

A coordinating **Codex Skill** for rigorous economics, environmental economics, regional economics, and empirical social-science writing. It complements existing research Skills rather than downloading their full repositories.

## Main capabilities

- **Economic Interpretation Gate:** Connect economic phenomena, research puzzles, candidate mechanisms, observable implications, evidence, and qualified policy implications.
- **Claim-level citation checks:** Usually 1–2 verified, directly relevant studies for a substantive economic judgment, or 1–3 complementary studies for a coherent mechanism cluster; these are guidelines, not mechanical citation quotas.
- **Evidence boundaries:** Distinguish a paper's direct findings from hypotheses supported only by outside literature.
- **Codex–Cursor independent review:** Separate scientific, economic-interpretation, and mechanism-citation quality gates.
- **Writing quality:** Coordinates seven existing research/writing Skills, two JMU guides and two substantive anti-defensive-writing passes.

## Installation

Clone or download this repository and put its contents in a folder named `social-science-writing-gate` under your project's `.agents/skills/` directory. The entry point is [SKILL.md](SKILL.md).

For installation and verification instructions, see [INSTALL.md](INSTALL.md).
